How It Works

  1. Submit a podcast RSS feed URL above.
  2. Copy the feed URL that appears and add it to your podcast app (Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts, etc.).
  3. The feed will show up with a ✂ prefix (e.g. "✂ The Daily") so you can tell it apart from the original.
  4. When you tap an episode, processing starts automatically. The first time, you'll hear a short "processing" clip instead of the episode.
  5. Come back a bit later and the ad-free version will be ready.

Each episode title has a symbol showing its status:

○ — Not yet processed (plays when you tap it)
◐ — Currently processing
● — Ready to listen (ads removed)
✘ — Something went wrong (will retry on next play)
Heads up: Podcast apps cache feeds aggressively and can be slow to show updates. After an episode finishes processing, it might take a while before your app refreshes and plays the ad-free version. Best to get episodes processing well ahead of when you actually want to listen. A pull-to-refresh in your podcast app can help speed things along.
